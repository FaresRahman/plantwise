# Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure — Press & Filling Lines

1. Purpose

This procedure defines the steps required to isolate hazardous energy before performing maintenance,
clearing a jam, or servicing equipment on the High-Precision Filler, Preform Injection press, or
Stretch-Blow Molding line. It applies to all Operators and Maintenance staff.

2. Scope

Applies to any task where a guard is removed, a jammed mechanism is cleared by hand, or an energized
component is accessed on: High-Precision Filler, Preform Injection, Stretch-Blow Molding, Dosing and
Dividing, and Pouch/Sachet Packaging lines.

3. Required PPE

Safety glasses, cut-resistant gloves, and steel-toe boots are required for all LOTO work. Hearing
protection is required on the Preform Injection line specifically due to compressor noise.

4. Isolation Steps

4.1 Notify the shift supervisor and the operator running the affected line before starting isolation.
4.2 Identify all energy sources for the equipment: electrical supply, pneumatic supply, and any stored
hydraulic pressure. The High-Precision Filler has a single electrical disconnect and one pneumatic
shutoff valve located on the east side of the machine frame.
4.3 Shut down the equipment using the normal stop control, then move the main disconnect to the OFF
position.
4.4 Apply your personal lock and tag to the disconnect. If more than one person is working on the
equipment, each person applies their own lock (group lockout).
4.5 Bleed any stored pneumatic or hydraulic pressure using the bleed valve before touching moving parts.
4.6 Verify isolation by attempting the normal start control — it must not run. Only after verification
is the equipment considered safe to service.

5. Restoring Power

5.1 Confirm all tools are removed and guards are reinstalled.
5.2 Confirm all personnel are clear of the machine.
5.3 Each person removes their own lock and tag — never remove another person's lock.
5.4 Notify the shift supervisor before re-energizing and restarting the line.

6. Exceptions

Minor adjustments that do not require removing a guard or reaching into a point of operation (e.g.
adjusting an external speed dial) do not require full LOTO, but the operator must still notify the
shift supervisor before proceeding.
