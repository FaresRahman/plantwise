# General Plant Safety SOP

1. Purpose

This SOP defines baseline safety rules for all personnel working on the production floor, covering
the High-Precision Filler, Pouch/Sachet Packaging, Preform Injection, Stretch-Blow Molding, and Dosing
and Dividing lines.

2. Personal Protective Equipment

Safety glasses and closed-toe shoes are mandatory on the production floor at all times. Hearing
protection is mandatory within 3 meters of the Preform Injection compressor. Cut-resistant gloves are
required when clearing jams or handling sharp packaging components.

3. Housekeeping

Aisles must be kept clear of pallets, hoses, and packaging waste. Spills (oil, product, or cleaning
chemicals) must be reported to the shift supervisor and cleaned up immediately using the spill kit
located at each line's control panel.

4. Reporting Hazards and Near-Misses

Any near-miss, damaged guard, exposed wiring, or malfunctioning e-stop must be reported to the shift
supervisor before the end of the shift, and the equipment must be tagged "Do Not Operate" until
inspected. Do not attempt to bypass a safety interlock or e-stop under any circumstance.

5. Working Near Moving Equipment

Never reach into a point of operation while a line is running. If a jam must be cleared, follow the
Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure before reaching into the machine. Loose clothing, jewelry, and lanyards
must be secured or removed near conveyors and the bottle unscrambler.

6. Emergency Stops

Every line has at least one e-stop within arm's reach of the operator position. On the High-Precision
Filler, e-stops are located at the infeed, the capper, and the case packer. Pressing any e-stop halts
the entire line — a supervisor must clear the fault and reset the e-stop before restart.

7. Shift Handover Safety Checks

At the start of each shift, the incoming operator must visually confirm all guards are in place, all
e-stops are unlatched and functional, and no "Do Not Operate" tags remain from the prior shift without
a documented resolution.
